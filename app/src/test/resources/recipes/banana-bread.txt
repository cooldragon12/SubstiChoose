Banana Bread

Ingredients
3 ripe bananas, mashed
1/3 cup melted butter
3/4 cup brown sugar
1 large egg, beaten
1 tsp vanilla extract
1 tsp baking soda
1/4 tsp salt
1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
1/2 cup chopped walnuts (optional)

Steps
1. Preheat the oven to 175°C and grease a loaf pan.
2. Mix the mashed bananas and melted butter in a bowl.
3. Stir in the sugar, egg and vanilla.
4. Sprinkle in the baking soda and salt, then fold in the flour and walnuts.
5. Bake for 55 to 60 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.
