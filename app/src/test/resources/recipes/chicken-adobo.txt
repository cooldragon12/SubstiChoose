Chicken Adobo

Ingredients
1 kg chicken thighs
1/2 cup soy sauce
1/3 cup cane vinegar
1 head garlic, crushed
3 bay leaves
1 tsp whole peppercorns
1 tbsp brown sugar
1 cup water

Steps
1. Marinate the chicken in soy sauce and garlic for 30 minutes.
2. Brown the chicken in a pot, then add the marinade, water, bay leaves and peppercorns.
3. Simmer covered for 30 minutes.
4. Add the vinegar and sugar; simmer uncovered for 10 minutes without stirring.
5. Serve with rice.
