Ginataang Kalabasa at Sitaw

Ingredients
500 g squash (kalabasa), cubed
1 cup string beans (sitaw), cut
200 g shrimp
2 cups coconut milk (gata)
1 tbsp bagoong alamang (shrimp paste)
1 onion, chopped
3 cloves garlic, minced
1 tbsp fish sauce (patis)
1 tbsp cooking oil

Steps
1. Saute the garlic and onion, then add the bagoong.
2. Pour in the coconut milk and bring to a simmer.
3. Add the squash and cook for 8 minutes.
4. Add the string beans and shrimp; cook for 4 minutes.
5. Season with fish sauce.
