Leche Flan

Ingredients
10 egg yolks
1 can (390 g) condensed milk
1 can (370 ml) evaporated milk
1 tsp vanilla extract
1 cup white sugar (for the caramel)
1 tsp calamansi zest (optional)

Steps
1. Melt the sugar in the llanera over low heat until golden; let it set.
2. Gently mix the egg yolks, condensed milk, evaporated milk and vanilla.
3. Strain the mixture into the llanera and cover with foil.
4. Steam for 35 to 40 minutes, until set.
5. Chill, then flip onto a plate.
