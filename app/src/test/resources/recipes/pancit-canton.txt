Pancit Canton

Ingredients
250 g pancit canton noodles
200 g pork, thinly sliced
150 g shrimp, peeled
2 tbsp oyster sauce
3 tbsp soy sauce
1 cup chicken broth
1 carrot, julienned
2 cups cabbage, sliced
1 cup snow peas
1 onion, sliced
4 cloves garlic, minced
2 tbsp cooking oil
Calamansi, to serve

Steps
1. Saute the garlic and onion in oil, then add the pork and cook until brown.
2. Add the shrimp, oyster sauce and soy sauce.
3. Add the vegetables and cook for 2 minutes; set aside.
4. Pour the broth into the pan, add the noodles and cook until absorbed.
5. Toss everything together and serve with calamansi.
