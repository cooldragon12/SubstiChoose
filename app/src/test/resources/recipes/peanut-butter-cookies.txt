Peanut Butter Cookies

Ingredients
1 cup peanut butter
1/2 cup butter, softened
1 cup brown sugar
1 egg
1 tsp vanilla extract
1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
3/4 tsp baking soda
1/2 tsp baking powder
1/4 tsp salt

Steps
1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
2. Cream the peanut butter, butter and sugar, then beat in the egg and vanilla.
3. Mix in the flour, baking soda, baking powder and salt.
4. Roll into balls, press with a fork, and bake for 10 minutes.
